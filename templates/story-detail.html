Sacrifice at Sundown

arvicki Studios Dec 2025 12 min read 100 Minted Passes

The sun dropped behind the jagged ridge like a spent brass casing. Dust hung suspended in the motionless air of Oakhaven, catching the amber twilight in an amber haze. For seven years, Cole had kept his hands off the steel, working the barren loam of his ranch with bleeding palms.

"A contract signed in blood can only ever be voided at high noon." — Cole Vance

Now three riders were waiting at the crossroads, their silhouettes stark against the burning red horizon. They had come not for vengeance, but to collect on a promise registered across the desert's only ledger.
